    Physics-Informed Complex-Valued Neural Networks for Ionospheric Scintillation Mitigation in L-band SAR Imagery
    Banon, F., Dell'aiera, M., et al. — International Symposium on Antennas and Propagation (ISAP), 2026.

    A physics-informed deep-learning approach to restore L-band SAR imagery (e.g. ALOS/PALSAR) degraded by ionospheric scintillation. A complex-valued neural network (CVNN) predicts the scintillation indices S4 and σφ from complex SAR data; a phase-screen physical model (Carrano et al., 2012) then regenerates the ionospheric transfer function, which guides a U-Net denoiser reconstructing the clean image. Training and stage-by-stage validation use a synthetic dataset with full physical ground truth.
