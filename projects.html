Relevant Aerospace and Computer Science Projects

  • Dynamical Simulation of the Asteroid Population
    IPSA, Paris, France
    • Modelled the motion of asteroid 2007 VW266 in retrograde resonance with Jupiter using Python.
    • Analysed asteroid perturbations and approximated orbital elements.
    • Simulated long-term asteroid trajectories to predict stability and future interactions with Jupiter.
    📄 Report ▶ Trajectory Plot (Video)
  • Neptune Flyby Mission
    IPSA, Paris, France
    • Calculated the trajectory and cost of a Neptune flyby mission, optimizing delta-V by integrating Jupiter flybys to minimize fuel consumption.
    • Used NASA's trajectory browser to plan interplanetary transfers and simulated orbital manoeuvres with focus on hyperbolic escape and gravity assists.
    • Delivered a full mission analysis covering orbit insertion, hyperbolic escape, and planetary encounter phases using STK.

Other Projects

  • Design and Simulation of a LH₂/LOX Combustion Engine
    Concordia University, Montreal, Canada
    • Designed injectors, combustion chamber and nozzle based on the F-1 engine using CATIA.
    • Established optimal mass flow rates for the fuel and oxidizer.
    • Simulated non-reacting flows, combustion and supersonic gas ejection using STAR-CCM+.
    • Studied mixing, temperature and combustion pressure; discussed geometry improvements to address pressure drops in the injector.
  • Aerodynamic Optimization of a Fixed-Wing UAV
    Concordia University, Montreal, Canada
    • Optimized wing design for environmental monitoring missions, improving lift-to-drag ratio and aerodynamic efficiency.
    • Conducted airfoil analysis using XFoil and performed 3D modelling in OpenVSP, integrating new root and tip airfoils with span extension and washout twist.
    • Applied the Vortex Lattice Method (VLM) for aerodynamic performance estimation.
  • Design and Test of a Turbine Blade using STAR-CCM+
    Concordia University, Montreal, Canada
    • Studied a first-stage cylindrical blade turbine cascade using STAR-CCM+.
    • Established the mesh and discussed simulation stability and accuracy.
    • Modelled flow in the cascade; analysed temperature, pressure profile and isentropic Mach number, with discussion of losses and stall risk.
