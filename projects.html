Relevant Space & Aerospace Projects

  • Dynamical Simulation of the Asteroid Population
    IPSA, Paris, France
    • Modelled the motion of asteroid 2007 VW266 in 13/14 retrograde mean-motion resonance with Jupiter, deriving the equations of motion from Keplerian to Cartesian coordinates with Sun and Jupiter perturbations.
    • Implemented the orbital propagation in Python with a 4th-order Runge-Kutta integrator, solving Kepler's equation via Newton-Raphson.
    • Ran Monte Carlo uncertainty propagation (orbit clones within the measurement error bounds) over 100 and 10,000 years to study the evolution of the orbital elements (a, e, i) and long-term stability, cross-validated against the Connors & Wiegert reference paper.
    📄 Report ▶ Trajectory Plot (Video)
  • Neptune Flyby Mission
    IPSA, Paris, France
    • Designed an Earth–Jupiter–Neptune flyby trajectory with the patched-conic method and a single Jupiter gravity assist, using the NASA Trajectory Browser solution as the design baseline.
    • Analytically computed each conic segment — circular parking orbit, hyperbolic Earth escape (≈ 7.92 km/s), Earth→Jupiter transfer ellipse, Jupiter gravity-assist geometry (≈ 11.6 km/s free heliocentric gain), and Jupiter→Neptune hyperbolic transfer.
    • Built the ΔV and mass budgets (Tsiolkovsky rocket equation) and reproduced the full trajectory in an STK12 simulation, with a transit time consistent with the NASA reference (~7 years).

Other Projects

  • Design and Simulation of a LH₂/LOX Combustion Engine
    Concordia University, Montreal, Canada
    • Designed injectors, combustion chamber and nozzle based on the F-1 engine using CATIA.
    • Established optimal mass flow rates for the fuel and oxidizer.
    • Simulated non-reacting flows, combustion and supersonic gas ejection using ANSYS and STAR-CCM+.
    • Studied mixing, temperature and combustion pressure; discussed geometry improvements to address pressure drops in the injector.
    📄 Report
  • Aerodynamic Optimization of a Fixed-Wing UAV
    Concordia University, Montreal, Canada
    • Optimized wing design for environmental monitoring missions, improving lift-to-drag ratio and aerodynamic efficiency.
    • Conducted airfoil analysis using XFoil and performed 3D modelling in OpenVSP, integrating new root and tip airfoils with span extension and washout twist.
    • Applied the Vortex Lattice Method (VLM) for aerodynamic performance estimation.
    📄 Report
  • Design and Test of a Turbine Blade using STAR-CCM+
    Concordia University, Montreal, Canada
    • Studied a first-stage cylindrical blade turbine cascade using STAR-CCM+.
    • Established the mesh and discussed simulation stability and accuracy.
    • Modelled flow in the cascade; analysed temperature, pressure profile and isentropic Mach number, with discussion of losses and stall risk.
